# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming and harassment‑free community.

## Our Pledge
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good intent; disagree without being disagreeable.
- No harassment, doxxing, or discrimination.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using inclusive language and offering constructive feedback
- Being mindful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Insults, trolling, or sexualized language
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may take appropriate action, including warning or banning, for any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Reporting
Report issues to **<boris@mrakbook.com>**. All good‑faith reports will be investigated and kept as confidential as possible.
